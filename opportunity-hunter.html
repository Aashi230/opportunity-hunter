How Match Scoring & Verification Work

Results are fetched live from public sources via TinyFish / Claude connector or loaded from verified program fixtures. Deadlines, locations, and eligibility are extracted directly from the actual text and explicitly categorized as found on page or not found.

Relevance scoring (100 pts max): Interest alignment (35 pts) • Skills overlap (25 pts) • Opportunity type (15 pts) • Location / work mode (15 pts) • Study year criteria (10 pts). Scores indicate profile alignment, not guaranteed admission.
